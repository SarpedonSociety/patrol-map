COMSUBPAC Patrol Plot

Trigger's convoy attack, as her captain drew it

USS Trigger (SS-237), sixth war patrol · 21–22 September 1943 · attacks 3 to 9 · 26-27 N, 122-40 E

Sources and notes rev. 5

How to read it

The drawing underneath is the original: a sketch inked into the narrative of the patrol report, Enclosure (A), page 8, with the note that the situation “can best be illustrated by the following sketch.” Everything that moves is laid over it, and the line style says where each part comes from.

Solid: the attack forms. Torpedoes, their number, the minute fired, and hit, miss or dud come from the Torpedo Attack Data forms, pages 27 to 40. Where each one runs is traced from the sketch. Dashed: the sketch. Ship positions, the tracks of the ships after they were hit, and Trigger's numbered firing positions are traced from the drawing. Dotted: inferred. Trigger's path between positions 7, 8 and 9 is not on the sketch. The dotted line only joins them, bringing her to each one pointing the way the sketch draws her.

Captions in italics are the report's own words, read from the scanned pages. Captions in upright type are summaries.

What is and is not known

  • Not to scale, and neither is the clock. The sketch is a recollection, not a plot. Firing ranges on the forms run from 800 to 2,200 yards, and when Tanker #2 blew up she was a glow “over the horizon, beyond the burning tanker.” The first four attacks took five minutes and the last three were spread over three hours, so the animation slows down for the first and skips ahead between the others. It also slows wherever a caption needs longer to be read.
  • One position for everything. All seven forms give 26-27 N, 122-40 E, so the sketch is the only record of where the ships were in relation to each other.
  • The sketch and the forms agree on direction. With north at the top, Trigger's course on each form matches the way the torpedoes are drawn to within about 30°, including the stern shots of attacks 5, 6, 8 and 9. Where they differ, the boat is pointed as the sketch has her. The convoy's course, 080° T, is drawn straight across the page.
  • How fast the ships moved along their tracks is inferred. The sketch shows where each ship went, not when. Movement is timed to fit the narrative.
  • Which torpedoes hit. The forms give only a count (“Two hits”). The animation follows the arrows on the sketch.
  • Freighter #3's track is a reading of the sketch. After attack 7 she wheels left through the position of attack 8, runs back east, doubles back across the line of the attack 8 torpedoes to the position of attack 9, and turns away to the south-west. The sketch gives the shape of that track; the narrative, page 10, has her getting underway and reversing course between attacks 8 and 9.
  • Track angle, attack 3. The narrative has “60 S track”; the form has an average of 80 starboard.
  • The asterisk. The note on the sketch belongs to Tanker #2, whose track bends around Tanker #1 and ends at “Tank burning” on the right. The ship drawn in flames below it is labelled “Burning Tank #1”.
  • Results are as claimed in the report, not as later assessed.
