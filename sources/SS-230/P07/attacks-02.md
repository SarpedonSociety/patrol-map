DETAILS OF ACTION

Page 15
at 2,200 yards with 4" gun using 4" common ammunition.  Closed range
to 900 yards.  Opened fire with three 20 mm guns and two air cooled
50 cal. machine guns at 1,800 yards when target opened fire with a
machine gun.  Obtained about eight hits with 4" gun, three of which
exploded.  Numerous hits were obtained with the small arms and the
enemy machine gun was silenced.  The small arms eliminated all per-
sonnel topside.  Broke off action when all 4" ammunition was expended.
Had we had a smooth sea, more hits would have been obtained, and the
target completely wrecked.  The lack of a foot firing device again
contributed to our inaccuracy of fire.  Sensitive nose ammunition is
much more effective than common ammunition against targets.
Ammunition expended:

  20 mm - 600 rounds
