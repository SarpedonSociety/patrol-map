               Details of Action
Conducted battle surface, closed range on surface from 4000 to
2100 yards, and opened fire on last sampan in column of six headed for
Nomwin Island, with 4" gun, three 20 MM machine guns, and 2 air cooled
50 caliber machine guns. The 4" gun fire was not as effective as us-
ual due to smallness of target, the targets movements, and the stiff-
ness of the foot firing device. The gun captain fired the gun on
pointer's "Fire". Stiffness of foot firing device was due to bending
of linkage in rough seas. The target was straddled continually. The
range varied between 2000 yards and 900 yards. Some shells of common
ammunition seemed to pass thru the target without exploding. About
ten 4" hits were observed in addition to those hits not exploding.
Broke off action with only 18 rounds of 4" ammunition left, and after
wrecking sampan experienced jams with all three 20 MM guns. These
jams were as follows: #1 20 MM broken firing pin; #2 20 MM defec-
tive ammunition; and #3 20 MM broken firing pin.
Fired Following Ammunition:
    42 rounds    4" H.C. (sensitive nose)
    30 rounds    4" Common

  1110 rounds    50 Caliber
