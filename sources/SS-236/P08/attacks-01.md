(I) MINES
None sighted.
(J) ANTI-SUBMARINE MEASURES AND EVASION TACTICS
In general, weak and ineffective. During the attack on the convoy, the
escorts became confused, dispersed the convoy, and did not interfere with
our attacks. The patrol boat which operates off TOAGEL MLUNGUI has a high
nuisance value but did not attack effectively after our attack on the
freighter near the reef. A prompt and well organized search for us after this
attack could have been embarrassing as our escape courses were limited. No
air patrols were sighted in vicinity of WAKE and TRUK and at PALAU only as
convoy coverage or after we had sounded off. Destroyers made high speed
sweeps off PALAU when convoys were arriving or departing. We were attacked
once by a submarine well south of PALAU. It is possible that the Jap is
now relying more on submarine as an anti-submarine weapon and this may explain
the lack of air patrols. By keeping their planes on the ground, more surface
patrolling is conducted, and better targets are offered for their submarines.
(K) MAJOR DEFECTS AND DAMAGE
# 1 Auxiliary Engine: - Crankshaft is frozen, probably due to the seizure
of the thrust bearing. About five minutes after starting on Jan. 13, 1944,
smoke was noticed between the engine and generator. Engine was immediately
secured. As smoke had an odor of burning insulation, the generator end was
first carefully checked over but conditions were found to be normal. It is
believed that # 8 or thrust bearing is seized. Attempts to remove thrust
bearing proved unsuccessful due to lack of special tools required. Engine
hours this patrol - 180. Engine hours since last overhaul, 388, at which
time the generator armature had been renewed.
-27-
Flags: Part (K) continues on the next page, which was not supplied. The word "Jap" has a blot over the last letter.
