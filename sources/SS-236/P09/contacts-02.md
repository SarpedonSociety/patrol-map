output. The second table is badly faded. The two OCR reads paired its latitudes and longitudes with the wrong rows
from 15 March on, so the rows below come from the scan: Claude read them from the PSO's clippings, and the PSO read
the latitudes of the 28 February, 29 February and 1 March rows by hand. Readings still uncertain are in ⟦ ⟧.

The time column is headed 'Time (GCT)'. A 2100 observation on Mar. 7 is therefore 0600 (I) on 8 March. Rows are in
the report's order. The 'Position' cell (latitude over longitude) is joined on one line. Where a row cannot be
reconciled with the narrative as typed, the row stays as typed here and the plotted estimate is in patrol.yml under
row_estimates, with the evidence in review.yml.
