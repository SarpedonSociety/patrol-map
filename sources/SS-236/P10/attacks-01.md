TARGET DATA - DAMAGE INFLICTED
Description:        Convoy was seen to leave PORT APRA morning of May 9th. Could not
                    close to firing position at this time but convoy was seen to
                    consist of 1 AP similar to TATUHARU MARU and six AK average

Ships probably sunk:   1 AK, 5,000 tons.

                    at large AP. Heard breaking up and sinking noises on two dis-
                    tinct bearings. When came to periscope depth saw MIKAGE MARU
                    burning and watched it sink. Saw sufficient debris in vicinity
                    to account for two other ships sunk. Convoy was turned back
                    and when seen later could only count three ships, one of which
                    appeared to be damaged.
