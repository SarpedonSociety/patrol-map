TORPEDO ATTACK REPORT FORMs, typed pages 11 to 14 of Enclosure (A) (microfilm pages 12 to 15), read from the scan by
Claude on 2026-10-19 from the JPGs supplied by the PSO. Not Gemini output. A Gemini read of these pages
(check-attacks-01.md) had the tube columns of attacks 2 and 3 scrambled (tubes #8 and #9 swapped in attack 2; every
serial, spread and depth in attack 3 out of order), invented "faint digit" flags for cells that are clear, and
misread the description of attack 1B as FURUKI. Each form is one page; it is split here into a target-data block and a
torpedo-data block so that the tube columns are read as a unit. Typed page numbers are used. Attack numbers ONE - A
and ONE - B are given as 1A and 1B.
