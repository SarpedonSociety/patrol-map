Parts (F) Ship contacts and (G) Plane contacts, typed pages 9 and 10 of the report (microfilm pages 10 and 11), read from
the scan by Claude on 2026-10-19 from the JPGs supplied by the PSO. Not Gemini output. A Gemini read of these pages
(check-contacts-01.md) had the plane table rows scrambled (rows 1-8 merged or misplaced, a spurious row 4), wrong
positions for rows 11 and 14 and an invented "All 3" for the tonnage. Page 10 is badly faded: the time, latitude and
longitude cells of rows 1 to 8 are largely lost to ink and were read from an enhanced copy of the scan; cells in ⟦ ⟧
are uncertain, and ⟦..⟧ stands for characters that could not be made out at all.
