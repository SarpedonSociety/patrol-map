with the PSO's hand readings of the Addenda form of 13 November 2026.

Layout changes, nothing else:
- Each form prints its target data and torpedo data on one page; they are split here under the two headings the
  converter expects, with the same page number. The first form's torpedo data run onto page 29 (the stern tubes).
- Qwen read the first form's tube-by-tube rows column by column; Bonsai read them row by row. The rows below follow
  Bonsai's order, and every figure agrees between the two reads.
- Both forms are typed 'Torpedo Attack No. ONE'. The Addenda's form is headed 'attack 2' here so the converter keeps
  the two apart; its Flags line says so.
- Addenda pages are numbered afresh: the Addenda form's 'Page 8' is the Addenda's page 8.
