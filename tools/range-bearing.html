Range & Bearing Plotter

Paste position entries from a patrol report, such as 40 miles, bearing 150T ASHIZURI SAKI. Each line is matched to a landmark and converted to latitude and longitude. One entry per line; lines starting with # are skipped.

The degree sign is optional: 150T, 150°T and 150 T all work. Distances in miles are read as nautical miles; yards are converted.
